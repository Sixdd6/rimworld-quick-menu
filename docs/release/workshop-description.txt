[h1]Quick Menu[/h1]
A wheel (or grid) of your favourite build and order buttons that opens around your mouse. Pin the things you place most, press one button, pick, and place.

[h2]Requires[/h2]
[list]
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2009463077]Harmony[/url]. Load it before Quick Menu.
[/list]
For RimWorld 1.7. Safe to add or remove mid-save: nothing is written to your saves.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Wheel or grid[/b], fixed or auto size. Set rings, columns, rows and button size.
[*][b]Pin from the architect menu[/b] with Ctrl+click on any build or order button.
[*][b]Material ring[/b] for buildings made from stuff.
[*][b]Dropdown sub-rings[/b] for pinned dropdowns such as floors and zones.
[*][b]Edit mode[/b]: click the centre button, drag slots to swap or move them, then click Done.
[*][b]Greyed-out pins[/b] when something is not available yet. The tooltip tells you why.
[*][b]Pin markers[/b] in the architect menu show what is already pinned.
[*][b]Missing mods[/b]: pins from mods you removed come back when you add the mod again. "Forget missing pins" clears them.
[/list]

[h2]How to use[/h2]
[list]
[*]Press [b]Mouse 4[/b] on the map to open the menu. No side button? Use [b]middle mouse[/b], or bind any key in the mod settings.
[*][b]Ctrl+click[/b] a button in the architect menu to pin it. Ctrl+click again to unpin. With Dubs Mint Menus loaded, use [b]Alt+click[/b].
[*]Click a pin to select it. Right-click or click outside the menu to close.
[*]Settings: Options > Mod settings > Quick Menu.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]Vanilla architect menu
[*]Better Architect Menu
[*]Dubs Mint Menus: pinning from its build menu and wheel
[/list]

[h2]Known limits[/h2]
[list]
[*]Dubs Mint Menus: no pin markers in its list view or wheel, and its designator bars are not supported.
[*]Better Architect Menu logs its own error "InitializeDubsMintMenusReflection: toggleDesMethod is null". That message is not from Quick Menu.
[*]Multiplayer is not supported yet. There is no Multiplayer build for RimWorld 1.7.
[/list]
